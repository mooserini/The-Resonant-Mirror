# Docs — The Resonant Mirror

This folder is the platform's paper. It exists because the work outgrew
the places it was living — a laptop Desktop, an agent's sandbox — and
needed a canonical home. As of October 29, 2026, this repository is
that home: the trunk. Other copies are working copies and backups,
refreshed from here.

## What lives here

- `consolidation-map.md` — the map of the platform and what absorbs
  into it, drawn October 28, 2026.
- `docs-home-sketch.md` — why the documents moved here, and the rules
  they moved under.
- `doctrine/getadongle-guardian-sketch.md` — the Guardian's next duty,
  sketched the night the rename was decided: Hermes Config Guardian
  has always been guarding getadongle, not Hermes alone.
- `../content/raves/` — Ransom Raves of the Resonant Mirror: the
  dark-side ledger. Systems that don't say what they mean, documented
  with receipts.

More collections arrive as they are absorbed: the legacy adapter
papers (scrubbed public derivatives), the Discord doctrine, the
Closed Loop sketch.

## What never lives here

- Unscrubbed audit originals. Custody copies — hash-verified, held in
  the sandbox and the Drive audit folder — are the internal record.
  What ships here is derived from them *after* scrubbing.
- Credentials, tokens, key material, or the system files of any
  running machine. The live board's home is its own machine; this
  repo may hold its papers, never its running body.

## The rule

Mean what you say and say what you mean. A document here states what
it is, what it covers, and what it does not — on its face, before
anyone has to dig.
